Source: "SVG playing cards" by Adrian Kennard (RevK), https://www.me.uk/cards/ (https://cards.revk.uk),
the same generator and licence as res/common/cards-svg. Each back is the 2B.svg ("red" back, the
redcolour pattern on backcolour) of one generator request, fetched 2026-10-01 and kept unmodified
here as <XP back id>_<name>.svg. The common query string is the faces' (res/common/cards-src/
GENERATOR_URL.txt) plus the back design and its two colours:

https://www.me.uk/cards/makeadeck.cgi?size=poker&ace=Fancy&ace1=&ace2=&qr=&value=0&super=1&pip=1&wider=on&zip=Download&back=<Design>&redcolour=%23<pattern>&backcolour=%23<background>

Notes:
- Goodall (56, 59, 64) ignores redcolour: the ornament is always black; Arrows (63) ignores both colours
  of its marks (multicolour). Maze (57, 62) and Arrows are drawn at random by the generator, so the
  same URL returns a different maze / scatter each time: the files here are the ones it returned.
- The XP id is cards.dll's back id: the registry's Back value is id - 53, the engine's back index id - 54
  (asset id 1200 + index, res/solitaire/solitaire.rc).
- Rasterised to res/solitaire/backs/<id>_<name>.png (400x560) by make_backs.py (tools/make_assets.sh
  backs), after an edit of ours (CC0 as well): the pattern block of Diamond, Illusion and Maze scaled to a
  5-unit inset so its colour reaches the 3-D pile edges, the Diamond lattice drawn at twice its period
  (moire at small sizes), and the edge band made opaque.
